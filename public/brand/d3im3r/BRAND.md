# Brand usage guidelines

**Name:** d3im3r  
**Symbol:** Owl-chip  
**Signature concept:** `{o⌄o}` (U+2304)

## Preferred treatments

- Light surfaces: `assets/svg/*-light.svg`
- Dark surfaces: `assets/svg/*-dark.svg`
- Small UI: `assets/svg/icon-*.svg` or `assets/favicon/favicon.svg`
- Full identity: `assets/svg/horizontal-*.svg` or `assets/svg/vertical-*.svg`

Keep the original proportions, color relationships, and visual features. The supplied SVGs are a vectorization of approved raster artwork; minor edge differences may exist.
